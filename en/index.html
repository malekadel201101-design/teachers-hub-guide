Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the platform and choosing a tutor.

Official FAQ ↗
Is Teachers Hub free for students?

According to its official FAQ, browsing tutor profiles is free. An eligible parent account is required to view available contact details.

Are lessons online or in person?

Both formats are supported. Check each tutor profile for the formats and service areas offered.

Are lesson payments processed by the platform?

No. The platform says students and tutors arrange lesson details and payments directly.

Are all tutors verified in exactly the same way?

The platform displays completed verification signals on each profile. Review the specific signals shown rather than assuming every profile has undergone identical checks.
